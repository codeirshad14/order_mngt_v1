How to install

Pick your device. The steps for the one you are on are shown first.

Your device

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install App above, or open the ⋮ menu and tap Install app / Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install. The icon appears on your home screen.
Your device

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Your device

Windows & Mac

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Install App above, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. The app opens in its own window and is added to the Start menu or Dock.

Questions

Is the app free?

Yes. There is nothing to pay and no subscription. You only need a dealer account to sign in.

Do I need the Play Store or App Store?

No. The app installs straight from this website, so there is no store, no sign-up and almost no storage used.

Which username and password do I use?

The same ones you use on the website. If you do not have an account yet, contact us and we will create one for you.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Every time you open the app it already has the latest prices, stock and features.

Does it work without internet?

The app opens and shows a clear message when you are offline. Placing orders and seeing live stock need a connection.

How do I remove it?

Like any other app: long-press the icon and choose Remove or Uninstall. On a computer, open the app's menu and choose Uninstall.
